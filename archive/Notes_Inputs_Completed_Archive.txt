Feature:5 : Completed
Positive Breakeven (+BE: Entry + 2% / +2 Ticks) Gain Lock on +10% Spikes
• Concept: When an option contract moves into substantial profit (High >= Entry + 10%), trail Stop-Loss to `Entry + 2%` or `Entry + 2 Ticks` (e.g. ₹5.85 on ₹5.75 entry) instead of flat Breakeven (₹5.75).
• Problem it Solves:
    In fast-reversing contracts (like KOTAKBANK 420 PE which spiked +13.9% to ₹6.55 before crashing), trailing to exact entry (₹5.75) resulted in a -₹900 loss after spread slippage when exiting at ₹5.35.
• Architecture & Files to Touch:
    1. `common/position_monitor.py`: Update `TRAIL-1 (+10% Gain Lock)` logic to set `new_sl = round(entry_s * 1.02, 2)` or `entry_s + max(0.10, tick_size * 2)` to guarantee green trade closure on reversal.
    2. `input/program_config.json`: Add `"trail_be_buffer_pct": 2.0` setting.
